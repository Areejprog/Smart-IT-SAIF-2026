Smart IT combines IT asset management, service ticket management, operational analytics, and risk assessment within a single platform.

Its intelligent component transforms operational data into an actionable risk assessment rather than only displaying raw records. The system calculates a risk score from multiple operational indicators, classifies the overall risk level, identifies the contributing factors, and generates recommended actions.

This approach provides IT teams with a more structured view of operational conditions and supports data-driven prioritization and preventive monitoring.

Future development will focus on:

• Machine learning-based predictive risk models
• Historical trend analysis and risk forecasting
• Automated alerts and notifications
• Stronger role-based access control
• Advanced asset-to-ticket relationships
• Mobile and cross-platform applications
• Integration with enterprise IT service management systems
• Enhanced cybersecurity monitoring and audit capabilities
